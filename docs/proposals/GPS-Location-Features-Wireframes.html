1. Overview

Five location features are proposed. Each one can be switched on independently by the Admin, and they are ordered so the simplest, most reliable features ship first.

After Mark Complete, the Visit record is the proof of visit.
  1. Check-in is recorded on confirming Start Repair; check-out on Mark Complete. Distances are measured from the customer's saved pin.
  2. The customer pin is saved on the first visit, because Indian addresses are often landmark-based.
  3. With weak GPS (basements, older buildings) the app takes the best fix within 20 seconds and marks it “Low accuracy” instead of blocking the job.
  4. On my way is optional; sharing stops automatically at check-in. Bench jobs at the shop skip the Visit section.

Opened from home. Uses on-duty location points only.

Tracking, allowance and retention rules.
  1. Every rate and threshold is a setting, so each shop can match its existing allowance policy.
  2. A 2-minute interval improves the live map but uses more battery; 5 minutes is a good default on budget phones.
  3. Raw location points are deleted after the retention period. Visit records stay with the job as proof of service.
  4. Branches can be added later, each with its own geofence.

6. Rollout, privacy and practicalities

Suggested rollout

PhaseFeatureScreensBackground location
1Geotagged check-in / check-out3, 6, 8No — one fix when a button is tapped
2Attendance by geofence1, 2, 6, 8No
3On-duty tracking2, 5, 8Yes
4Distance and travel allowance4, 6, 8Yes

Phases 1 and 2 need only a single location reading when the technician taps a button, so they work reliably on budget phones and raise few privacy concerns. Phases 3–5 need background tracking, a foreground notification, and battery-setting guidance, so they follow once the basics are proven.

India-specific practicalities

TopicApproach
Budget Android phonesForeground service with a visible notification, plus brand-specific battery-setting help (Screen 1).
Patchy mobile dataPoints are stored on the phone and uploaded when the network returns; job actions never wait for GPS.
Data and battery useAbout 1–5 MB of data per month at a 5-minute interval; a few percent of battery per day.
Landmark addressesSave the customer's pin on the first visit and reuse it.
Maps costGoogle Maps by default; Mappls (MapmyIndia) or Ola Maps are cheaper alternatives if usage grows.
DPDP Act, 2023Clear notice and purpose, duty hours only, a retention limit, and technicians can see their own data.
