What TEZVOFlow covers

The field day, in one system

Built for HVAC, plumbing, electrical, facilities, and any team that takes a van to a job.

Smart dispatch

Assign by skill, parts on the van, travel time, and SLA — not a whiteboard and a WhatsApp group.

Live GPS & ETA

See where every technician is. Customers get an honest arrival window, automatically.

Technician app

Today’s route, job history, checklists, photos, and parts — usable with one thumb, including offline.

Work orders

Fault codes, safety steps, required photos, and customer sign-off on the same screen.

Van stock & parts

Know what’s on the truck before you send someone. Reorder when a job consumes the last filter.

Invoice on site

Quote extras, take a signature, send the bill, and collect UPI or card before the van moves.

Customer updates

SMS / WhatsApp: “we’re on the way”, “tech has arrived”, “job complete — here’s your invoice”.

SLA & first-time fix

Watch callbacks, travel waste, and jobs that miss the window — so the next week is tighter.
