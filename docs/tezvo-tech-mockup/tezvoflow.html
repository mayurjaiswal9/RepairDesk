How it works

From a WhatsApp message
to a closed job.

No double entry, no lost messages. Every step updates the same work order, so everyone sees what is happening in real time.

01

Customer books

They message on WhatsApp and pick an open slot — no app to install.

02

Office plans

The job lands on the planner. Drag it to the right technician and time.

03

Technician works

Job details, directions and checklist arrive on the technician's phone.

04

Job closed

Photos, signature and invoice are captured on site. The customer is updated.

WhatsApp booking

  • Reminders and “technician on the way” updates

Questions

Frequently asked.

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp or email support@tezvo-tech.com.

Do my customers need to install an app?

No. Customers simply message you on WhatsApp as they do today. Only your office team and technicians use TEZVOFlow.

Can it work with more than one branch?

Yes. You can run multiple planners and teams from one account, with the work order as the single source of truth.

Can TEZVOFlow be customised for our services?

Yes. TEZVOFlow is built by our team in Hyderabad, so we can tailor job types, checklists, forms and invoices to how you work.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on your team size and the modules you need. After a short walkthrough we share a clear, written quote.
